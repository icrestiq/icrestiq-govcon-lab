The problem nobody tells small businesses

SAM.gov — the government's contracting portal — is a maze built for insiders. Most small business owners open it once, get overwhelmed, and never come back.

Meanwhile, over $170 billion in federal contracts went to small businesses last year alone. Set-aside programs (8(a), HUBZone, women-owned, veteran-owned) reserve contracts specifically for businesses like yours — but only if you know how to find them and show up correctly.

You're not losing to bigger competitors. You're losing to confusion.
